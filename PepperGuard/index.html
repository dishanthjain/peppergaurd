    Rollout path

    Hardware readiness

    1. PrototypeESP32 + SHT31/DHT22 sensor inside a ventilated probe enclosure.
    2. PilotCalibrate against reference instruments; compare sensor placement across sack stacks.
    3. ValidationWork with post-harvest scientists to validate thresholds and sampling intervals.
    4. ProductionAdd a secure API, device registry, SMS/WhatsApp alerts and audit logs.
    Safety boundary

    PepperGuard predicts environmental fungal-spoilage risk. It does not detect Aspergillus, aflatoxin or product safety. Suspected contamination requires inspection and accredited laboratory testing.
